## Hello, I'm Vinay Ketkar

I hail from **Ujjain**, a historic city in Madhya Pradesh, India — famous for the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga and the Kumbh Mela.

### My Career

After completing my engineering studies, I began my professional journey at **Godrej & Boyce Manufacturing Company**, where I spent a couple of formative years learning the foundations of industrial engineering and equipment manufacturing.

I then moved to **Larsen & Toubro Ltd. (L&T)**, one of India's largest and most respected engineering conglomerates, where I built my career over **27 years**. During my time at L&T, I worked across multiple domains:

- **Application Engineering** — Understanding customer requirements and translating them into technical solutions for heavy engineering products
- **Marketing** — Driving business development and customer relationships for industrial and infrastructure equipment
- **Information Technology** — Contributing to L&T's IT subsidiary, bridging the worlds of engineering and technology

### Retirement & Beyond

Now retired, I've traded boardrooms for open roads and deadlines for daylight. My days are filled with:

- 📚 **Reading & reviewing books** — across genres, from technology to philosophy
- 🌍 **Travelling** — exploring India, the USA, Canada, and beyond
- 📷 **Photography** — capturing landscapes, people, and moments
- 🎨 **Art & crafts** — expressing creativity through various mediums
- 🌱 **Gardening** — nurturing plants and enjoying the rhythm of nature
- 🔧 **DIY projects** — the engineer in me never truly retires!

### This Website

This is my personal space on the internet where I share travel stories, book reviews, photographs, creative projects, and anything interesting I come across. I write in **English**, **Hindi**, and **Marathi**.

Feel free to explore and leave your comments on any blog post — I'd love to hear from you!
