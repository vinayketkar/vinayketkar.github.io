## Exploring the World

Travel has been one of the greatest joys of my retirement. From the vibrant temples of South India to the vast national parks of North America, every journey brings new perspectives, friendships, and stories.

Here you'll find travel stories accompanied by photographs — each post capturing the essence of a destination, its people, and its culture.

🇺🇸 **USA** — The diverse landscapes and cultures of America, from coast to coast.
