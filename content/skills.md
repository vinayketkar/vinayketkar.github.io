## Professional Expertise

Over nearly three decades in the engineering industry, I developed deep expertise across several domains:

### Marketing of Heavy Engineering Products

My primary forte at Larsen & Toubro was the marketing and business development of heavy engineering products. This involved:

- Understanding complex industrial requirements across sectors — power, infrastructure, process industries, and more
- Building and nurturing long-term customer relationships
- Presenting technical solutions to diverse audiences — from plant engineers to C-suite executives
- Market analysis and competitive positioning of capital equipment

### Application Engineering

Before a product reaches the customer, it needs the right technical fit. My work in application engineering involved:

- Translating customer needs into detailed technical specifications
- Selecting and configuring equipment for specific industrial applications
- Collaborating with design teams to ensure product-market fit
- Preparing technical proposals and documentation

### Equipment Usage Guidelines & Servicing

Ensuring customers get the best out of their equipment is just as important as selling it:

- Formulating comprehensive usage and maintenance guidelines for heavy machinery
- Training customer teams on safe and efficient equipment operation
- Coordinating after-sales service and support
- Troubleshooting and resolving field issues

### Information Technology

In my later years at L&T, I contributed to the IT subsidiary, bringing an engineer's perspective to technology:

- Bridging the gap between traditional engineering processes and modern IT solutions
- Supporting digital transformation initiatives

*These skills were honed over 2 years at Godrej & Boyce and 27 years at Larsen & Toubro Ltd.*
