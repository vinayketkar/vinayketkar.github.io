Namaste and welcome!

After nearly three decades in the engineering industry and now enjoying the freedom of retirement, I've decided to create this little corner of the internet to share my thoughts, experiences, and discoveries.

## What You'll Find Here

**Book Reviews** — I've always been an avid reader. From technical literature during my working years to a much broader palette now, I enjoy exploring different genres and sharing my impressions. Expect honest reviews and recommendations.

**Travel Stories** — Retirement has given me the gift of time, and I've been using it to explore. From the ancient temples of India to the national parks of the USA and the wilderness of Canada, I have many tales and photographs to share.

**Interesting Finds** — Life has a way of presenting us with fascinating topics when we least expect it. Whether it's an unusual piece of history, a clever innovation, or simply a thought-provoking idea, I'll share whatever catches my curiosity.

## Languages

I write in **English**, **हिन्दी (Hindi)**, and **मराठी (Marathi)**. You can switch between languages using the language selector in the navigation menu.

## Join the Conversation

I'd love to hear from you! Feel free to leave comments on any post — agree, disagree, share your own experiences, or simply say hello.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you find something here that resonates with you.
